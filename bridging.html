What It Is

Protection Where Work Happens Overhead

When construction or facade work takes place above people and property, overhead bridging keeps everyone below safe. Ambedo designs and installs protective decks and bridges that span entrances, driveways, alleys and adjacent roofs — engineered for the exact loads and conditions of tight NYC sites.

We pair bridging with debris netting, catch platforms and enclosure systems so nothing falls where it shouldn't, keeping your site clean, compliant and neighbor-friendly.

  • Heavy-duty load-bearing bridges
  • Entrance and lobby protection canopies
  • Roof and adjacent-property protection
  • Debris netting and catch platforms
