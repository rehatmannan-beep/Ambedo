How We Build

Watch Your Project Come Together

From bare sidewalk to full protection in days. Scroll down to see how Ambedo Holdings builds your sidewalk shed or pipe scaffold from the ground up.

Step 1: Foundation

Base Plates & Mudsills

Every project starts with a solid foundation. Our crews set base plates and mudsills on the sidewalk, establishing the footprint for the entire structure.

Step 2: Uprights

Vertical Standards Rise

Pipe scaffold uprights are set into the base plates and plumbed perfectly vertical. These steel standards carry the entire load of the structure above.

Step 3: Framing

Ledgers & Transoms Connect

Horizontal ledgers and transoms lock the uprights together, creating the rigid framework that gives sidewalk sheds and scaffolding their strength.

Step 4: Bracing

Cross Braces for Stability

Diagonal cross braces are added throughout the structure, preventing racking and ensuring the scaffold can withstand wind loads and dynamic forces.

Step 5: Platforms

Decking & Work Surfaces

Scaffold planks and platform decking go in, creating safe working surfaces for your crews and overhead protection for pedestrians below.

Step 6: Complete

Guardrails, Lights & Inspection

Guardrails, toeboards, and lighting are installed. Our team performs a final safety inspection. Your project is ready — DOB-compliant and built to last.
