What We Do

Complete Scaffolding & Sidewalk Protection

From ground-up supported scaffolds to pedestrian sidewalk sheds and overhead bridging, Ambedo delivers turnkey access and protection systems engineered for New York City job sites.

Supported Scaffolding

Engineered frame and system scaffolds that give your crews safe, stable access at any height — for façade work, restoration and new construction.

  • Frame & tube-and-clamp systems
  • Façade restoration & Local Law 11
  • Custom heights & load ratings

Sidewalk Sheds

Code-compliant sidewalk sheds (bridging) that keep pedestrians safe and keep your project open during construction, demolition and repairs.

  • Heavy & light-duty canopies
  • Lit, ADA-clear walkways
  • Permit & DOB filing support

Overhead Bridging & Protection

Overhead protection decks and bridging that shield walkways, entrances and adjacent structures from falling debris throughout the build.

  • Debris netting & catch platforms
  • Entrance & roof protection
  • Heavy-duty load bridging
