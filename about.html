Our Story

NYC Sidewalk Sheds & Pipe Scaffolding Done the Right Way

Ambedo Holdings was built to do two things exceptionally well: sidewalk sheds and pipe scaffolding across New York City. We help general contractors, developers and building owners get safe, DOB-compliant pedestrian protection and access scaffolding up fast — from single brownstones to full-block Local Law 11 restorations — and we manage it through the entire project.

We started Ambedo because too many projects were slowed down by unreliable crews, missed permits and safety corners cut. Our answer is simple: show up on time, engineer it right, file the paperwork, and stand behind the work. That's it.

Sidewalk sheds and pipe scaffolding are our core services. As we grow with our clients, we're expanding into construction fencing, debris chutes and temporary access — becoming a single, dependable partner for site protection in NYC.
