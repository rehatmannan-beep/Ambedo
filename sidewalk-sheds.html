What It Is

Pedestrian Protection That Keeps You Open for Business

A sidewalk shed — often called sidewalk bridging — is a temporary overhead canopy that shields pedestrians from falling debris during facade work, construction and demolition. In New York City, it's required for most work above a certain height and under Local Law 11/FISP when a facade is unsafe.

Amguard builds sheds that do their job without disrupting yours: clear, well-lit walkways that keep storefronts visible and foot traffic moving, engineered to the exact duty rating your project needs.

  • Heavy-duty (construction) and light-duty (parapet) sheds
  • Lit, ADA-clear walkways that keep your entrance open
  • Custom lengths, heights and column spacing
  • Optional protective fencing, signage and branding wrap
