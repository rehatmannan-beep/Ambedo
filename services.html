Access Systems

Supported Scaffolding

Stable, engineered frame and tube-and-clamp scaffolds that give your crews safe access at any height.

Whether you're restoring a landmark façade, performing Local Law 11/FISP repairs or building new, our supported scaffolding is designed to your load requirements and installed to keep your project moving safely.

  • Frame & system scaffolds

    Configured for façade, restoration and new construction work.

  • Engineered load ratings

    Light, medium and heavy-duty configurations to spec.

  • Local Law 11 / FISP

    Façade inspection and repair access done right.

Pedestrian Protection

Sidewalk Sheds (Bridging)

Code-compliant sidewalk sheds that protect pedestrians and keep your building accessible during construction.

Required by the NYC DOB for most work above a certain height, our sidewalk sheds are built to keep foot traffic safe below while work continues above. We handle heavy and light-duty canopies, lighting and clean, ADA-clear walkways.

  • Heavy & light-duty sheds

    Rated for construction loads and material storage where needed.

  • Lit, ADA-clear walkways

    Safe, well-lit passage that keeps your storefront open.

  • DOB permit filing

    We handle the paperwork so you stay compliant.

Overhead Protection

Overhead Bridging & Protection

Protective decks and bridging that shield walkways, entrances and neighboring structures from falling debris.

When work happens overhead, our bridging and overhead protection systems keep everyone below safe. We install heavy-duty load bridges, entrance canopies and roof protection engineered for your site conditions and duration.

  • Load-bearing bridges

    Spanning entrances, alleys and adjacent roofs.

  • Entrance & roof protection

    Keep lobbies, doors and neighbors protected.

  • Engineered for your span

    Custom designs for tricky, tight urban sites.

  • Debris & dust netting

    Full enclosure options for restoration work.

  • Catch platforms

    Engineered platforms that intercept falling material.

  • Inspection-ready

    Installed to keep your site compliant and audit-ready.
