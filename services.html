Primary Service

Sidewalk Sheds (Bridging)

Code-compliant sidewalk sheds that protect pedestrians and keep your building accessible while work happens above.

Required by the NYC DOB for most work above a certain height and under Local Law 11/FISP, our sidewalk sheds keep foot traffic safe below while your project continues. We manage heavy and light-duty canopies, lighting, clean ADA-clear walkways, permits and DOB filing — start to finish.

  • Heavy & light-duty sheds

    Rated for construction loads and material storage where needed.

  • Rental, maintenance & removal

    We manage the shed for the life of your project and remove it cleanly.

  • DOB permit filing

    We handle the paperwork so you stay compliant.
