What It Is

Safe, Stable Access at Any Height

Supported scaffolding is built up from a rigid base on the ground, giving your crews a solid working platform for facade restoration, masonry, painting, waterproofing and new construction. Amguard engineers each scaffold to the loads and reach your project demands.

Whether you're servicing a landmark facade, tackling a Local Law 11/FISP repair or building from the ground up, we design, install and maintain scaffolding that keeps your team safe and your schedule intact.

  • Frame, tube-and-clamp and system scaffolds
  • Light, medium and heavy-duty load ratings
  • Stair towers, hoists and access platforms
  • Debris netting and enclosure options

Supported Scaffolding Questions

Supported scaffolding is a scaffold built up from the ground on a rigid base of frames, tubes or system components. It gives workers safe, stable access to a building's exterior for facade work, restoration, painting and new construction.
We install frame (fabricated) scaffolds, tube-and-clamp scaffolds and system scaffolds, configured for light, medium or heavy-duty loads depending on your project requirements.
