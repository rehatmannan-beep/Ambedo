Everything you need to know about scaffolding, sidewalk sheds, bridging, permits and pricing in New York City. Still have a question? We're a phone call away.

Ambedo Holdings specializes in sidewalk sheds (sidewalk bridging) and pipe scaffolding across New York City — coordination, installation, rental management, maintenance and removal for Local Law 11, construction, emergency protection and building maintenance projects. We're also expanding into construction site fencing, debris chutes, and scaffolding & temporary access systems.
We serve all five boroughs of New York City — Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, The Bronx and Staten Island — plus select surrounding areas. Contact us with your address to confirm.
NYC generally requires a sidewalk shed for construction, demolition or facade work on buildings over a certain height, or when a facade is deemed unsafe under Local Law 11/FISP. We help confirm the requirement for your project and file with the DOB.
Cost depends on length, height, duty rating, site conditions and how long the structure stays up. Ambedo Holdings provides free, itemized quotes with no hidden fees.
